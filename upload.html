One-time setup needed

The uploader isn't connected yet — you can still add photos by pasting Cloudinary (or any) links directly into your Sheet's image_url column. To turn on drag-and-drop uploads here too:

  1. Sign up free at cloudinary.com and note your Cloud name (shown on the Dashboard).
  2. Go to Settings → Upload, scroll to "Upload presets," and click Add upload preset.
  3. Set Signing Mode to Unsigned, save, and note the preset name.
  4. Open assets/js/config.js and fill in CLOUDINARY_CONFIG.cloudName and CLOUDINARY_CONFIG.uploadPreset.
